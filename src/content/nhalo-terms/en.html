3. What NhàLo does

NhàLo is a tool for landlords and the people they appoint as managers. It helps you:

  • Manage houses, rooms, tenants and leases.
  • Record electricity and water meter readings per room and per period.
  • Calculate rent and create monthly invoices with a bank-transfer QR code (VietQR).
  • Send invoices to tenants via Zalo or email when you tap send.
  • Track what has and has not been paid, and get reminders.

Tenants do not install the app and do not have accounts. They only receive invoices via Zalo or email.

We may add, change or remove features to improve the service. If a major change removes a feature you rely on, we will tell you in the app in advance.
